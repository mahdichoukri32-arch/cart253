## Description

This prototype is inspired by the well-known dinosaur game created by Google : Chrome Dino. You will control a creature using your mouse; it is represented by a string. You’ll come across obstacles along the way. The further you get in the game, the harder it gets to control the creature. The obstacle isn’t longer in the environment but in your relationship with your steed. Do you want to set him free or force him ?
